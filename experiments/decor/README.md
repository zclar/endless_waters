# Decorations and items — 0.6.0

174 additions, with isolated `endless:decor_*`, `endless:food_*` and harpoon IDs.
Existing diving equipment and four building blocks retain their identifiers.

Decorations appear in Creative construction search. In Survival, put the listed
material into a **stonecutter** and select the desired output. Foods use the
crafting table with the ingredients listed below. Harpoon: two iron ingots and
two sticks in the recipe-book pattern. Throw to attack; recover its dropped item
after impact. Creative throws do not refund items. It does not return to your hand.

Interact with supported furniture to cycle shapes or toggle lights; sneak and
interact to change colors. Tables have manually selectable connection shapes.
Seats, cabinets, appliances, aquariums and nautical machinery are decorative;
they do not provide seating, inventories, cooking, fish capture or ship movement.
Foods are edible using vanilla ingredients, without a new crop system.
